About this plant

One of Lou’s mature ash trees overlooking the lake and distant hills.

Light

Full sun

Water

Established trees usually need supplemental water only during extended drought

Soil

Adaptable, but best in well-drained soil

Fertilizer

Usually unnecessary for a healthy mature tree

Pruning

Remove dead or hazardous limbs; use a qualified arborist for high work

Watch for

Emerald ash borer, canopy thinning, bark splitting and dead upper branches

Lou’s note: Because emerald ash borer is established in the region, inspect mature ash trees regularly and consider an arborist assessment.
