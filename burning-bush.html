About this plant

Lou’s mature multi-stem burning bush.

Light

Full sun to part shade

Water

Moderate; drought tolerant once established

Soil

Adaptable, well-drained soil

Fertilizer

Usually little or none

Pruning

Thin or shape in late winter before growth starts

Watch for

Euonymus scale, spider mites and unwanted seedlings

Lou’s note: Burning bush is considered invasive in parts of the Northeast. Avoid letting seedlings spread into nearby woodland.
