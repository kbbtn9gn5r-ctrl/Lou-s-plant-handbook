Tomato

Vegetable care from planting through seasonal cleanup.

Seed starting

Start indoors 6–8 weeks before the average last frost. Provide warmth and strong light; pot up once seedlings develop true leaves.

Planting outdoors

Harden off for 7–10 days and transplant after frost danger has passed and nights are reliably mild. Plant deeply and space for airflow.

Summer care

Give full sun, steady moisture, mulch, and sturdy support. Feed modestly once flowering begins and avoid excessive nitrogen.

Problems to watch

Inspect lower leaves for spotting, remove diseased foliage, and watch for hornworms, cracking, and blossom-end rot.

Harvest

Pick when fruit reaches full color and gives slightly. Green fruit can be brought indoors before frost.

Fall cleanup

Remove vines and fallen fruit after frost. Discard diseased material rather than composting it, and clean cages or stakes.
