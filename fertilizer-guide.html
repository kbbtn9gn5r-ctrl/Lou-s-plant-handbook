Tomato-Tone

Use for tomatoes, peppers, pumpkins, zucchini and winter squash according to the package rate. Water after application and keep fertilizer away from stems.

Flower-Tone

Use for dahlias, petunias and other flowering ornamentals. Avoid overfeeding perennials that already grow vigorously.

10-10-10

A general-purpose fertilizer, not automatically needed everywhere. Use only when soil or plant growth indicates a balanced feed is appropriate.

Acid-Loving Plant Fertilizer

Best suited to blueberries and rhododendrons when soil pH and growth indicate a need. Do not apply lime around these plants.

Compost

The safest default soil amendment for most beds, berries, trees and shrubs. Apply a light layer without piling it against trunks or crowns.

Important: Product labels control the actual rate and timing. Avoid combining several fertilizers at full rate.
