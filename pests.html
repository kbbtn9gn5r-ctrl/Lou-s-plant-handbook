🪲

Cucumber beetles

Check squash, pumpkin and cucumber plants often. Hand-pick when practical, protect young plants, and follow product labels exactly.

🥚

Squash bug eggs

Look for bronze egg clusters on leaf undersides. Remove or crush them before they hatch.

🐛

Squash vine borer

Watch for sudden wilting and sawdust-like frass near the stem base. Early detection gives the best chance of saving the vine.

⚪

Powdery mildew

Improve airflow, water at soil level, remove badly affected leaves, and avoid crowding.

🐌

Slugs

Reduce damp hiding places, inspect at dusk, and use barriers or labeled controls when needed.

🟢

Aphids

Spray off with water, encourage beneficial insects, and use insecticidal soap only as directed.

🐛

Tomato hornworm

Large green caterpillars can blend almost perfectly into tomato foliage and stems. Look for rapid defoliation, clipped stems, dark droppings, and missing leaves. Hand-pick when found. If a hornworm is covered with white parasitoid-wasp cocoons, leave it in place so the beneficial wasps can complete their life cycle.
