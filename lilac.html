About this plant

The large multi-stem shrub surrounded by daylilies.

Light

Full sun

Water

Deep watering during drought

Soil

Well-drained soil; avoid overly acidic, soggy sites

Fertilizer

Compost in spring; avoid heavy nitrogen

Pruning

Prune immediately after flowering. Remove some oldest stems at ground level.

Common problems

Powdery mildew, lilac borers, scale

Lou’s seasonal note: Photograph the flowers next spring to confirm the lilac type.
