Garden Phlox

Flower / Perennial care from planting through seasonal cleanup.

Planting

Grow in full sun to light shade with fertile, evenly moist soil and good airflow.

Spring care

Thin shoots when crowded and divide old clumps every few years.

Summer maintenance

Water at soil level, mulch, and deadhead to extend flowering. Watch for powdery mildew.

Fall cleanup

Cut stems after frost and remove infected foliage to reduce overwintering disease.
