Hosta

Flower / Perennial care from planting through seasonal cleanup.

Planting

Plant in part shade to shade in moisture-retentive, well-drained soil. Keep crowns at soil level.

Spring care

Clear winter debris before shoots elongate. Divide crowded clumps in spring if needed.

Summer maintenance

Water during dry periods, mulch lightly, and remove damaged leaves. Watch for slugs and deer.

Flowering

Remove spent flower stalks unless seed is desired.

Fall cleanup

Cut foliage after it yellows or after frost. Remove diseased leaves and refresh mulch lightly.
