Oregano

Culinary herb growing in Lou's garden.

Light

Full sun is best, about six or more hours daily.

Water

Water when the upper soil begins to dry. Oregano prefers good drainage and should not stay soggy.

Harvest & Pruning

Pinch and harvest stem tips regularly to keep the plant bushy. For strongest leaf flavor, harvest before heavy flowering.

Maine Seasonal Care

Keep the crown well drained. After winter, trim away dead stems when new spring growth starts.

Soil & Feeding

Use average, well-drained soil. Avoid excessive fertilizer, which can produce lush growth with weaker flavor.

Watch For

Root rot in wet soil; occasional aphids or spider mites.
