Potatoes

Near harvestWatch vine decline
Open full care details

Harvest signal: Main-crop potatoes are ready after vines yellow and die back naturally.

Before digging: Reduce watering as vines decline and choose a dry harvest day.

Curing: Cure unwashed potatoes in darkness with airflow for roughly 1–2 weeks.

Storage: Store only sound, dry tubers in a cool, dark place.

🎃Pest watch

Pumpkins & Winter Squash

Vines are growing and setting fruit.

☀ Full sun💧 Deep watering
Open full care details

Inspection: Check leaf undersides and stems for squash bug eggs at least weekly.

Removal: Scrape or tape off egg masses before they hatch.

Water: Water deeply at soil level and avoid routinely wetting foliage.

Fruit care: Keep developing fruit off wet soil and avoid damaging vines.

Watch for: Cucumber beetles, squash bugs, vine borers and powdery mildew.
