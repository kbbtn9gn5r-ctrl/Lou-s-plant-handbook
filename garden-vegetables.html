🟣Garden Vegetables

Beet

Dual-purpose crop grown for roots and greens.

☀ Full sun💧 Even moisture
Open full care details

Fertilizer: Avoid excess nitrogen

Harvest or pruning: Roots at about 1.5–3 inches

Common problems: Leaf miners, scab, poor rooting from crowding

Lou's notes: Thin seedlings early for good-sized roots.

🧄Garden Vegetables

Garlic

Hardneck garlic for midsummer harvest and curing.

☀ Full sun💧 About 1 inch weekly until leaves yellow
Open full care details

Fertilizer: Compost at planting; light nitrogen in spring

Harvest or pruning: Plant in fall; harvest when 4–5 lower leaves brown

Common problems: Onion maggots, basal rot, excess moisture

Lou's notes: Stop watering as harvest approaches. Cure in shade with strong airflow.

Hubbard Squash

Long-season winter squash with vigorous vines.

☀ Full sun💧 Consistent deep watering
Open full care details

Fertilizer: Tomato-Tone or comparable vegetable fertilizer

Harvest or pruning: Harvest after rind hardens, before a hard frost

Common problems: Cucumber beetles, vine borers, powdery mildew

Lou's notes: Cure before storage and protect fruit from direct soil contact.

Large vining crop with high water and nutrient needs.

Open full care details

Fertilizer: Tomato-Tone during active growth and fruiting

Harvest or pruning: Harvest when rind is hard and stem begins to cork

Red-skinned potato planted May 4 for summer harvest.

☀ Full sun💧 Even moisture during flowering and tuber set
Open full care details

Fertilizer: Balanced fertilizer; avoid excess nitrogen

Harvest or pruning: New potatoes after flowering; main crop after vines die back

Tomato

Warm-season crop needing support, steady water and regular feeding.

☀ Full sun💧 Deeply 1–2 times weekly at soil level
Open full care details

Fertilizer: Tomato-Tone at planting and through fruiting

Harvest or pruning: Harvest when fully colored and slightly soft

Common problems: Early blight, hornworms, blossom-end rot

Lou's notes: Mulch plants and avoid splashing soil onto leaves.

Winter Squash

Storage squash grown through summer and cured for winter use.

☀ Full sun💧 Deep, consistent watering while vines and fruit develop
Open full care details

Fertilizer: Tomato-Tone or another vegetable fertilizer at labeled rates

Harvest or pruning: Harvest when the rind is hard and before a hard frost

Common problems: Cucumber beetles, squash bugs, vine borers, powdery mildew

Lou's notes: Cure in a warm, dry location before moving fruit into cool storage.
