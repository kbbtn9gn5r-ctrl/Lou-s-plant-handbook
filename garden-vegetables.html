🟣Garden Vegetables

Beet

Beta vulgaris

🏠 Start indoors: No 📅 Direct sow Apr 20–May 20; again Jul 15–Aug 10
Planting and care details

Planting depth: ½ inch

Plant spacing: 2–4 inches

Row spacing: 12–18 inches

Soil temperature: 50–70°F

How to plant: Thin early; keep soil evenly moist. Sow every 2–3 weeks for a longer harvest.

Harvest: Harvest roots at 1½–3 inches; greens can be picked earlier.

Common problems: Leaf miners, scab, crowding

🧄Garden Vegetables

Garlic

Allium sativum

🏠 Start indoors: No 📅 Plant cloves Oct 10–Nov 5
Planting and care details

Planting depth: 2 inches, pointed end up

Plant spacing: 6 inches

Row spacing: 12–18 inches

Soil temperature: Cool fall soil

How to plant: Mulch 4–6 inches after planting. Remove scapes in early summer for larger bulbs.

Harvest: Harvest when 4–5 lower leaves are brown; cure 2–4 weeks.

Common problems: Onion maggots, basal rot, excess moisture

Herbs

Various species

🏠 Start indoors: Varies 📅 Basil indoors Apr 1–15; parsley Mar 15–Apr 1; dill/cilantro direct sow May
Planting and care details

Planting depth: Varies

Plant spacing: 6–18 inches

Row spacing: 12–24 inches

Soil temperature: Varies

How to plant: Most herbs need full sun and modest feeding. Harvest frequently before flowering.

Harvest: Pick in the morning after dew dries.

Common problems: Aphids, mildew, overwatering

🟦Garden Vegetables

Hubbard Squash

Cucurbita maxima

🏠 Start indoors: Yes 📅 Start indoors May 10–20; transplant Jun 1–10
Planting and care details

Planting depth: 1 inch

Plant spacing: 3–4 feet

Row spacing: 6–8 feet

Soil temperature: 70°F+

How to plant: Use biodegradable pots to reduce root disturbance. Harden off 7 days. Give vines abundant room.

Harvest: Harvest when rind is hard and stem corky, before hard frost.

Common problems: Cucumber beetles, vine borers, powdery mildew

🫘Garden Vegetables

Jade String Bean

Phaseolus vulgaris

🏠 Start indoors: No 📅 Direct sow May 25–Jul 10
Planting and care details

Planting depth: 1 inch

Plant spacing: 3–4 inches

Row spacing: 18–24 inches

Soil temperature: 60°F+

How to plant: Do not plant in cold wet soil. Sow another row every 2–3 weeks through early July.

Harvest: Pick slender pods every 2–3 days.

Common problems: Bean beetles, slugs, leaf spots

🥬Garden Vegetables

Kale

Brassica oleracea var. sabellica

🏠 Start indoors: Optional 📅 Start indoors Mar 20–Apr 10; transplant Apr 25–May 15; fall sow Jul 1–20
Planting and care details

Planting depth: ¼–½ inch

Plant spacing: 12–18 inches

Row spacing: 18–24 inches

Soil temperature: 45°F+

How to plant: Tolerates frost. Use row cover early to reduce cabbage moth damage.

Harvest: Harvest outer leaves first; flavor improves after frost.

Common problems: Cabbage worms, flea beetles, aphids

🫑Garden Vegetables

King of the North Pepper

Capsicum annuum

🏠 Start indoors: Yes 📅 Start indoors Mar 15–Apr 1; transplant Jun 1–10
Planting and care details

Planting depth: ¼ inch indoors

Plant spacing: 18 inches

Row spacing: 24–30 inches

Soil temperature: 70–85°F germination; warm soil outdoors

How to plant: Use heat for germination, strong light, and harden off 7–10 days. Do not rush into cold soil.

Harvest: Pick green or allow fruit to ripen red.

Common problems: Aphids, sunscald, blossom-end rot

Lettuce

Lactuca sativa

🏠 Start indoors: Optional 📅 Start indoors Mar 20; direct sow Apr 15–May 31 and again Aug 1–25
Planting and care details

Planting depth: ⅛–¼ inch

Plant spacing: 6–10 inches

Row spacing: 12–18 inches

Soil temperature: 40–65°F

How to plant: Succession sow every 10–14 days in cool weather. Provide afternoon shade in summer.

Harvest: Harvest outer leaves or cut full heads before bolting.

Common problems: Slugs, aphids, bolting

🎃Garden Vegetables

Pumpkin

Cucurbita pepo/maxima

🏠 Start indoors: Yes 📅 Start indoors May 10–20; transplant Jun 1–10, or direct sow after soil warms
Planting and care details

Planting depth: 1 inch

Plant spacing: 3–5 feet

Row spacing: 6–10 feet

Soil temperature: 70°F+

How to plant: Use biodegradable pots. Harden off carefully. Plant into rich soil and mulch after soil warms.

Harvest: Harvest when rind resists a fingernail and stem begins to cork.

Red Pontiac Potato

Solanum tuberosum

🏠 Start indoors: No 📅 Plant seed potatoes Apr 25–May 15
Planting and care details

Planting depth: 3–4 inches

Plant spacing: 12 inches

Row spacing: 30–36 inches

Soil temperature: 45°F+

How to plant: Hill soil when plants are 6–8 inches tall and again as needed. Keep tubers covered from light.

Swiss Chard

Beta vulgaris var. cicla

🏠 Start indoors: Optional 📅 Start indoors Mar 25–Apr 10; direct sow Apr 20–Jun 15
Planting and care details

Planting depth: ½ inch

Plant spacing: 8–12 inches

Row spacing: 18 inches

Soil temperature: 50°F+

🍅Garden Vegetables

Tomato

Solanum lycopersicum

🏠 Start indoors: Yes 📅 Start indoors Mar 25–Apr 10; transplant Jun 1–10
Planting and care details

Planting depth: ¼ inch indoors; transplant deeply

Plant spacing: 18–30 inches

Row spacing: 3–4 feet

Soil temperature: 70–80°F germination; warm soil outdoors

🌿Garden Vegetables

Rhubarb

Rheum × hybridum

🏠 Start indoors: No📅 Plant crowns in early spring or fall
Planting and care details

Planting depth: Set crown buds at soil level or about 1 inch below

Plant spacing: 3–4 feet

Row spacing: 4–5 feet

Soil: Rich, deep and well drained

How to plant: Plant crown divisions in amended soil. Keep the crown from being buried too deeply.

Harvest: Pull mature stalks in spring. Do not eat the leaves. Stop heavy harvest by early summer.

Common problems: Crown rot, weak growth from overcrowding, flower stalks
