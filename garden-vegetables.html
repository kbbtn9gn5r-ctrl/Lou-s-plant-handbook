Vegetable Garden

Named crops, harvest notes, and seasonal care.

Tomatoes

Keep evenly watered, tie new growth to supports, and feed according to the saved Tomato-Tone schedule. Watch lower leaves for spotting and remove only clearly diseased foliage.

Borlund and Red Pontiac Potatoes

Maintain an even moisture level while plants are flowering. Harvest new potatoes after flowering or wait until tops yellow and die back for mature potatoes.

Return to 2026 photos

King of the North Pepper

Provide full sun, steady moisture, and support if fruit becomes heavy. Avoid excessive nitrogen once plants are flowering.

Hubbard Squash and Cucumbers

Guide vines into open space, water deeply at soil level, and check leaves and blossoms for cucumber beetles and squash pests.

Return to 2026 photos

Detroit Beets

Thin crowded roots, water consistently, and harvest selected roots when they reach the preferred size while leaving smaller plants to continue growing.

Return to 2026 photos

Hardneck Garlic

Harvest when several lower leaves have browned while upper leaves remain green. Cure with strong airflow for roughly two to three weeks before trimming and storage.

Sunflowers

Provide full sun and support tall stems if exposed to wind. Protect developing seed heads from birds when needed.
