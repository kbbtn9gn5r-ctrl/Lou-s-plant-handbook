Japanese Pieris

Acid-loving evergreen shrub with attractive foliage and spring flower clusters.

Light

Part shade or morning sun is ideal. A sheltered site helps protect foliage from harsh afternoon sun and winter wind.

Water

Keep soil evenly moist but never waterlogged, especially while the shrub is establishing.

Soil

Use acidic, organic, well-drained soil similar to conditions favored by rhododendrons and blueberries. Mulch over the shallow root zone.

Pruning

Prune lightly just after flowering when shaping is needed. Avoid heavy late-season pruning.

Maine Care

Choose a protected location, maintain mulch, and water before the ground freezes if autumn is unusually dry.

Watch For

Lace bugs, leaf spot and winter burn. Check leaf undersides if foliage becomes stippled or pale.

Safety Note

All parts of Japanese Pieris are toxic if eaten, so keep clippings away from pets and livestock.
