Boomer & Trixie and the Tuesday Lunch Adventure

A Tuesday family tradition at Lou and Peg’s house.

Every Tuesday, Boomer and Trixie know something special is about to happen.

They don’t need a calendar. Somehow, they just know that Tuesday means family is coming for lunch.

Peg was already busy in the kitchen. She had a healthy lunch planned, with plenty of organic fruit, and one very important favorite on the menu: cheeseburgers. Lillian and Carter loved cheeseburgers.

Lou wandered into the kitchen to check on things.

“Is it ready yet?” he asked.

Peg smiled. “Lou, they aren’t even here yet.”

Lou was always ready to eat. Boomer, sitting hopefully nearby, seemed to agree.

Then a car pulled into the driveway.

Boomer’s ears went up. Trixie jumped to her feet.

The door opened, and Lillian came in first with Carter.

Boomer exploded with excitement. He bounced toward them, tail wagging so hard his whole body wiggled. Trixie hurried over too, trying to be a little more sensible.

“Easy, Boomer,” Lou reminded him.

Boomer listened for about three seconds.

Trixie gave him the look. “Boomer, you’re gonna get in trouble.”

A little while later Taylor arrived carrying baby Jacoby, and the excitement started all over again.

Once everyone settled in, Lillian found some games to play while Carter went straight for his trucks.

“Vroom! Vroom!”

A truck rolled across the floor.

Boomer’s eyes followed it.

Trixie noticed immediately. “Don’t.”

The truck rolled past again.

Boomer chased it.

“Boomer!” Lou called.

Boomer stopped. Carter laughed. Trixie looked at Boomer as if to say, I told you so.

Then Peg announced lunch, and everyone gathered around the table. The cheeseburgers were a hit with Lillian and Carter, and Peg made sure there was plenty of healthy food and organic fruit to go with them.

Boomer and Trixie took up their usual positions nearby: sit quietly, look adorable, and hope somebody drops something.

Boomer watched Carter very carefully.

“Boomer,” Lou warned.

Boomer sat a little straighter.

After lunch, Lou helped Peg with the dishes while Boomer and Trixie supervised.

When the kitchen was cleaned up, everyone gathered by the window. Birds were visiting the yard, and then a hummingbird appeared, darting from one spot to another.

Lillian and Carter watched closely. Taylor held Jacoby nearby. Even Boomer and Trixie stared out the window, their heads turning as the hummingbird zipped back and forth.

Then Lou asked, “Who wants to see what’s ready in the garden?”

Lillian and Carter were ready immediately.

They headed outside with Boomer and Trixie walking right along with them toward the garden. Lou, Peg and Taylor followed, with Taylor carrying Jacoby.

In the garden, Lillian and Carter looked over the vegetables to see what might be ready to eat. Boomer and Trixie sniffed along the path.

Boomer suddenly discovered something very interesting between two plants.

He stuck his nose in.

“Boomer,” Trixie warned.

He went farther.

“Boomer...”

Only Boomer’s back end was showing when something rustled. He jumped backward with a leaf stuck to the top of his head.

Lillian laughed. Carter laughed. Lou looked over.

“What are you doing, Boomer?”

Boomer stood perfectly still, pretending nothing had happened.

Trixie stared at him. “You never listen.”

By the time everyone walked back to the house, the Tuesday visit had included cheeseburgers, organic fruit, games, trucks, birds, a hummingbird, a garden walk and just enough Boomer trouble to make everybody laugh.

Later, Boomer curled up beside Trixie.

“That was a good Tuesday,” he said.

Trixie agreed.

“Do you think they’re coming back next Tuesday?” Boomer asked.

Trixie stared at him. “Boomer. They come every Tuesday.”

Boomer’s tail began wagging.

Across the room Lou smiled. He was already thinking about next Tuesday’s lunch.

And that was the best part of Tuesdays at Lou and Peg’s house: family around the table, children laughing, dogs underfoot, birds outside the window, a walk through the garden, and another memory waiting to be made.

The End 🐾
