About this plant

Lou’s mature maple beside the driveway.

Light

Full sun to part shade

Water

Deep water during prolonged drought

Soil

Moist, well-drained soil

Fertilizer

Usually unnecessary unless growth or a soil test indicates a deficiency

Pruning

Remove dead or crossing branches in late summer or while fully dormant

Watch for

Tar spot, verticillium wilt, storm damage and girdling roots
