Spider Plant

Complete indoor maintenance guide for Spider Plant.

Light

Provide the light level described for the plant and rotate the pot for even growth.

Watering

Check the potting mix before watering. Water thoroughly, allow drainage, and never leave the pot standing in water.

Feeding

Feed lightly during active growth and reduce or stop during slow winter growth.

Repotting & Propagation

Repot when roots crowd the container. Use a well-draining mix appropriate for the plant and propagate from divisions, offsets, or cuttings as appropriate.

Seasonal Maintenance

Remove damaged growth, inspect for pests, clean leaves, and adjust watering as indoor light and temperature change.
