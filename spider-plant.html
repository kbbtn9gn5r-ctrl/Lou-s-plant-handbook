Spider Plant

Complete indoor maintenance guide for Spider Plant.

Light

Bright indirect light is ideal. Gentle morning sun is usually fine.

Watering

Water when the upper portion of the potting mix begins to dry. Let excess water drain completely.

Feeding

Feed lightly during active growth and reduce feeding in winter.

Plantlets

The hanging baby plants can be rooted in small pots once they have developed enough growth.

Seasonal Maintenance

Trim brown tips as needed, remove damaged foliage, and rotate the pot for even growth.
