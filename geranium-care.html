Geranium

Complete indoor maintenance guide for Geranium.

Light

Provide bright light with several hours of sun for compact growth and good flowering.

Watering

Water thoroughly, then allow the upper potting mix to dry before watering again.

Feeding

Feed during active growth and flowering, but avoid overfeeding with nitrogen.

Pruning

Deadhead spent flower clusters and pinch back leggy stems to encourage branching.

Seasonal Maintenance

Inspect regularly for pests and adjust watering as indoor light changes.
