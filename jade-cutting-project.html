Jade Pruning & Cutting Project

Mother plant recovery, new branching, and individually potted cuttings.

Jade Plant

The mother jade was structurally pruned on June 29, 2026. The current photographs show fresh shoots developing near the cut tips and the plant beginning to fill out again.

The removed stems were allowed to callus and then planted in individual pots. Keep them in bright light, avoid frequent watering, and wait until the potting mix dries before watering again.
