Potatoes

Vegetable care from planting through seasonal cleanup.

Planting

Plant certified seed potatoes in cool spring soil. Cut large pieces so each has at least one good eye and allow cut surfaces to dry briefly.

Growing

Hill soil or mulch around stems as they grow to protect developing tubers from light. Keep moisture even during flowering and tuber formation.

Feeding

Use a balanced, moderate fertilizer; too much nitrogen produces foliage at the expense of tubers.

Harvest

Harvest new potatoes after flowering. For storage potatoes, wait until vines yellow and die back, then allow skins to set before digging.

Storage

Cure in a dark, ventilated place, then store cool and dark. Do not store green or damaged tubers.

Fall cleanup

Remove vines and missed tubers to reduce disease and volunteer plants next year.
