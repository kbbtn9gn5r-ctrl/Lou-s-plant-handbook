LOU'S GARDEN GUIDE — SMALLER UPDATE SYSTEM (VERSION 2.1)

What changed
------------
The Projects page and Garden Journal now read their cards from one file:

  site-content.json

This means most future additions do NOT require uploading the entire website.

Typical future journal update
-----------------------------
Upload only:
1. The new photograph(s)
2. site-content.json

Typical future project-card update
----------------------------------
Upload only:
1. The project's new photograph(s)
2. site-content.json
3. The individual project page, only when that page itself changed

Files that normally stay unchanged
----------------------------------
- projects.html
- journal.html
- site-content-renderer.js
- style.css
- most plant pages and old photographs

Important
---------
Upload patch files into the main GitHub repository folder and choose Replace when a file already exists. Do not delete old photographs unless they are known duplicates or incorrect.
