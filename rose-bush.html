About this plant

Lou's established rose bush from the earlier garden photographs.

Light

At least 6 hours of direct sun

Water

Deeply at soil level

Soil

Rich, well-drained soil

Fertilizer

Rose fertilizer or compost in spring and after the first bloom

Pruning

Prune in early spring and deadhead during the flowering season.

Common problems

Black spot, Japanese beetles, aphids
