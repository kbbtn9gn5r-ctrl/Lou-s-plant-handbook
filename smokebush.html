About this plant

The large shrub with pink, cloud-like flower plumes and rounded foliage.

Light

Full sun

Water

Moderate; drought tolerant once established

Soil

Well-drained soil

Fertilizer

Usually little fertilizer is needed

Pruning

Prune in late winter for foliage growth or after bloom to preserve flowers.

Common problems

Leaf spot, verticillium wilt, winter dieback

Lou’s seasonal note: Avoid severe pruning if the goal is to preserve the smoky flower display.
