STORY 7

Boomer and Trixie Go to the Garden and Find the Rabbit

📚 All Stories

It was a beautiful morning, and Lou was heading out to the garden. The sun was warm, the birds were singing, and Boomer and Trixie were already waiting by the door.

“Want to come to the garden?” Lou asked.

Boomer was through the door before Lou finished the question. Trixie followed at a much more sensible pace.

As soon as they reached the garden, Boomer’s nose went to work. He sniffed along the tomatoes, checked beneath the squash leaves, and investigated every interesting smell he could find.

Trixie stayed close to Lou, watching Boomer carefully.

Then Boomer stopped.

His ears went up. His nose pointed toward a row of vegetables.

Trixie saw the look on his face.

“Boomer,” she warned. “You’re gonna get in trouble.”

Boomer took one slow step forward.

Something rustled beneath a big green leaf.

Boomer froze.

Trixie froze.

Lou looked over.

And then a little rabbit popped out from beneath the plants.

For one second, nobody moved.

Boomer stared at the rabbit. The rabbit stared at Boomer. Trixie stared at Boomer because she already knew what was coming next.

The rabbit suddenly hopped across the garden.

Boomer took off after it.

“BOOMER!” Lou called.

Trixie ran after him, barking as if to say, “I told you! I told you!”

The rabbit slipped safely through the garden fence and disappeared into the tall grass.

Boomer stopped at the fence and looked through it, completely amazed.

Lou caught up with him. “We don’t chase the rabbits, Boomer.”

Boomer sat down and tried his very best to look innocent.

Trixie walked over and sat beside him.

She gave Boomer that familiar look.

“You’re gonna get in trouble.”

Boomer looked at Trixie, then back toward the place where the rabbit had disappeared.

On the walk back to the house, Boomer kept looking over his shoulder at the garden.

Trixie finally asked, “What are you looking for?”

Boomer wagged his tail.

“I’m just wondering if the rabbit will be here tomorrow.”

Trixie sighed.

Something told her that tomorrow’s trip to the garden was going to be very interesting.
