Sunflower

Flower / Vegetable Companion care from planting through seasonal cleanup.

Planting

Direct sow after frost in full sun. Space according to mature height and protect emerging seedlings from birds.

Growing

Water during establishment, then deeply during dry periods. Stake tall varieties in exposed locations.

Flower care

Deadhead ornamental types for appearance or leave heads for seed and wildlife.

Seed harvest

When backs of heads turn yellow-brown and seeds are plump, cover heads if birds are taking them, then cut and dry.

Fall cleanup

Remove stalks after seed harvest or leave selected heads for winter birds.
