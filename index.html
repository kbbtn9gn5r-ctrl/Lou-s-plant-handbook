Pest & disease guide

Fast reference for common problems in Lou's garden.

🪲

Cucumber beetles

Check squash, pumpkin and cucumber plants often. Hand-pick when practical, protect young plants, and follow any product label exactly.

🥚

Squash bug eggs

Look for bronze egg clusters on leaf undersides. Remove or crush them before they hatch.

🐛

Squash vine borer

Watch for sudden wilting and sawdust-like frass near the stem base. Early detection gives the best chance of saving the vine.

⚪

Powdery mildew

Improve airflow, water at soil level, remove badly affected leaves, and avoid crowding plants.

🐌

Slugs

Reduce damp hiding places, inspect at dusk, and use barriers or labeled controls when needed.

🟢

Aphids

Spray off with water, encourage beneficial insects, and use insecticidal soap only as directed when populations persist.
