Annual Flowers

Seasonal flowering plants grown for continuous summer color.

☀ Full sun to part shade, depending on variety💧 Consistent moisture, especially in containers
Open full care details

Fertilizer: Flower-Tone or regular bloom fertilizer

Harvest or pruning: Deadhead and trim leggy growth through summer

Common problems: Aphids, budworms, root rot, heat stress

Lou's notes: Add individual annual varieties as they are photographed and identified.

🌺Flowers & Outdoor

Dahlia

Summer-flowering tuber grown for large, colorful blooms.

☀ Full sun💧 Deeply, about 1 inch weekly
Open full care details

Fertilizer: Flower-Tone or another low-nitrogen bloom fertilizer

Harvest or pruning: Deadhead all season; lift tubers after frost

Common problems: Aphids, slugs, earwigs, powdery mildew

Lou's notes: Stake tall plants early and thin crowded center stems for airflow.

🌻Flowers & Outdoor

Daylily

Reliable perennial with arching foliage and summer flowers.

☀ Full sun to part shade💧 About 1 inch weekly while establishing
Open full care details

Fertilizer: Compost or light balanced feed in spring

Harvest or pruning: Remove spent scapes; divide crowded clumps

Common problems: Aphids, spider mites, daylily rust

Lou's notes: Each bloom lasts one day, but established clumps produce many buds.

💜Flowers & Outdoor

Lavender

Fragrant perennial requiring sun and excellent drainage.

☀ Full sun💧 Allow soil to dry between watering
Open full care details

Fertilizer: Little fertilizer needed

Harvest or pruning: Prune lightly after flowering; avoid cutting old wood

Common problems: Root rot, winter injury, poor drainage

Lou's notes: A gravelly, well-drained site improves winter survival.

🌸Flowers & Outdoor

Penstemon

Upright flowering perennial valued by pollinators.

☀ Full sun💧 Moderate water in well-drained soil
Open full care details

Fertilizer: Light compost; avoid excessive fertilizer

Harvest or pruning: Deadhead for appearance and leave some seed heads if desired

Common problems: Root rot, powdery mildew, slugs

Lou's notes: Good drainage is especially important for overwintering.

🌸Flowers & Outdoor

Peony

Long-lived perennial with fragrant late-spring flowers.

☀ Full sun💧 Deeply during dry spells
Open full care details

Fertilizer: Light Flower-Tone after growth begins

Harvest or pruning: Cut spent flowers; remove foliage after frost

Common problems: Botrytis blight, ants on buds, flopping stems

Lou's notes: Do not bury the crown too deeply.

Pollinator Plants

Flowering plants selected to support bees, butterflies and other beneficial insects.

☀ Usually full sun💧 Water during establishment and extended drought
Open full care details

Fertilizer: Compost or light feeding based on the species

Harvest or pruning: Stagger bloom periods and leave some seed heads for wildlife

Common problems: Aphids, mildew, browsing animals

Lou's notes: Favor a mix of spring, summer and fall bloomers.
