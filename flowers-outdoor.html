Dahlias

Plant after frost danger in rich, well-drained soil and full sun. Stake tall plants early, water deeply, deadhead regularly, and lift tubers after frost for winter storage in Maine.

Hosta

Water during dry periods and remove spent flower stalks when appearance declines.

Petunias

Deadhead or trim leggy stems, water when the top soil begins to dry, and feed regularly during active bloom.

Liatris

Provide full sun and well-drained soil. Leave some seed heads for wildlife or cut spent spikes for a tidier bed.

Lilies

Remove spent flowers but leave green stems and leaves until they yellow naturally so bulbs can recharge.

Garden Phlox

Water at soil level, provide air movement, and remove faded flower clusters to extend the display.

Sedum

Use well-drained soil and avoid overwatering. Leave dried flower heads for winter interest or remove them in spring.

Daylilies

Grow in full sun to light shade. Divide crowded clumps in spring or after flowering. Remove spent stalks, leave healthy foliage through summer, and cut foliage back after frost.
