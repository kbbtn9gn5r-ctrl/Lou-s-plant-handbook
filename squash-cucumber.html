Cucumbers and Hubbard Squash

Vegetable care from planting through seasonal cleanup.

Seed starting

Direct sow after soil warms, or start indoors 3–4 weeks before transplanting. Avoid disturbing roots.

Planting

Give full sun, rich soil, and generous spacing. Trellis cucumbers when practical; allow Hubbard squash room to run.

Summer care

Water deeply at soil level, mulch, and monitor blossoms for pollination. Guide vines away from paths.

Pests

Watch closely for cucumber beetles, squash bugs, vine borers, and powdery mildew.

Harvest

Pick cucumbers frequently. Harvest Hubbard squash when the rind is hard, color is mature, and stems begin to cork.

Fall cleanup

Remove vines and fruit debris promptly. Do not compost heavily diseased vines.
