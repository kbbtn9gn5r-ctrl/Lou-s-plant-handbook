Lily

Flower / Bulb care from planting through seasonal cleanup.

Planting

Plant bulbs in fall or spring in sun to light shade, with roots cool and tops in light. Use well-drained soil.

Spring care

Mulch lightly and protect emerging shoots from browsing.

Summer maintenance

Water deeply during dry weather, support tall stems, and remove spent flowers without cutting leafy stems.

After bloom

Allow foliage to yellow naturally so bulbs recharge.

Fall cleanup

Cut stems only after they have browned. Remove diseased foliage.
