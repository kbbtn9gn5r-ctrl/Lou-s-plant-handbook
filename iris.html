Iris

Perennial iris care for Lou's garden.

Light

Grow in full sun for best flowering. Most bearded iris perform best with at least six hours of direct sun.

Soil & Water

Use well-drained soil. Water during establishment and prolonged dry spells, but avoid keeping rhizomes continually wet.

Planting & Division

Keep bearded iris rhizomes at or just above the soil surface. Divide crowded clumps after flowering, usually midsummer in Maine.

Seasonal Care

Remove spent flower stalks. Leave healthy foliage during the growing season, then clean away dead or diseased foliage in fall.

Watch For

Inspect for iris borers, soft rot, leaf spot, and poor flowering caused by shade or overcrowding.
