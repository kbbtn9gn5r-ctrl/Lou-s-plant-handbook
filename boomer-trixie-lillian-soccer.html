🐾 Boomer and Trixie: Lillian's Two-Goal Soccer Adventure! ⚽

It was a beautiful Saturday morning when Pop announced, “Come on, everybody! We're going to Lillian's soccer game!”

Boomer immediately jumped off the couch. “WOOF! WOOF! WOOF!”

Trixie looked up from her comfortable blanket. “Oh, brother,” she thought. “Here we go again.”

Nini packed water, snacks, and two little dog treats. Pop grabbed the folding chairs, and off they went. Boomer sat in the back seat, his little brown nose pressed against the window. Trixie, the sensible white dog, settled comfortably beside him.

“Today,” Boomer seemed to say, “I'm going to be Lillian's assistant coach!”

Trixie rolled her eyes. “Just try not to embarrass us.”

⚽ The First Goal!

When they arrived, Lillian was already warming up with her teammates. “LILLIAN!” Pop shouted, waving. Lillian spotted Boomer and Trixie and waved back.

Boomer went absolutely bananas! He jumped up and down, wagged his tail, and barked so loudly that three parents turned around.

“Boomer!” Nini whispered. “Use your inside voice!” But Boomer didn't HAVE an inside voice at soccer games.

The referee blew the whistle. Lillian raced down the field. She stole the ball from another player, dribbled around one defender, then another.

Pop stood up. “GO, LILLIAN! GO!” Lillian pulled back her foot and kicked. WHOOSH! The ball sailed right past the goalie and into the net!

“GOOOOOOAL!” Pop threw both hands in the air. Nini cheered, and Boomer started barking like he'd just won the World Cup.

Trixie stood up, wagged her tail twice, and gave one very polite bark. “Woof.” That was Trixie's version of a standing ovation.

🐕 Boomer Joins the Team!

At halftime, Pop took the dogs for a little walk beside the field. Everything was going perfectly until Boomer spotted something: a SOCCER BALL! It had rolled off the field and was sitting all alone in the grass.

Before Pop could say a word, Boomer grabbed his leash in his mouth and charged toward it. “BOOMER! COME BACK!”

Boomer reached the ball and began pushing it with his nose. Bump! Bump! Bump! He pushed it straight toward the field!

“Oh, for heaven's sake!” Nini laughed. Trixie watched from beside Pop. “I don't know that dog,” her expression seemed to say.

Lillian came running over. “BOOMER! ARE YOU TRYING TO PLAY SOCCER?” Boomer proudly put one paw on the ball. “WOOF!”

Lillian laughed so hard she nearly fell over. “Sorry, Boomer. You're not on my team!” Pop retrieved the ball, and Boomer reluctantly returned to his spot beside Nini.

🏆 The Winning Goal!

The second half began, and the score was tied. Lillian's team needed one more goal.

With only two minutes remaining, Lillian received a beautiful pass from her teammate. She sprinted toward the goal. One defender approached. Lillian moved left. Another defender came charging. Lillian moved right.

Boomer was practically vibrating with excitement. Trixie sat perfectly still, watching every move.

Lillian took one final step and kicked the ball with all her might. BOOM! The ball flew toward the upper corner of the net. The goalie jumped. She stretched out both hands. But she couldn't reach it!

GOOOOOOOOOOAL! LILLIAN HAD SCORED AGAIN! TWO GOALS! And her team had taken the lead!

Pop jumped out of his chair so fast he nearly knocked over his coffee. “THAT'S MY GIRL!” Nini clapped and cheered.

Boomer let out the loudest bark anyone had ever heard from such a little dog. “WOOOOOOF! WOOF! WOOF!”

And then something amazing happened. Trixie joined in! “WOOF! WOOF!” For once, the two dogs agreed on something. Lillian was AMAZING!

❤️ The Celebration

When the final whistle blew, Lillian's team had WON! Lillian came running toward Nini and Pop.

“DID YOU SEE MY TWO GOALS?”

“SEE THEM?” Pop exclaimed. “I nearly lost my voice cheering!” Nini gave Lillian a great big hug.

Boomer immediately tried to jump into the celebration. He licked Lillian's hand, bounced around her feet, and wagged his tail so hard his entire little body wiggled.

Trixie calmly walked over and gave Lillian a gentle kiss on the ankle. “Good job,” her little face seemed to say.

Then Boomer spotted something sticking out of Lillian's soccer bag. HER SOCK! Before anyone could stop him, he grabbed it and took off across the grass.

“BOOMER! GIVE ME MY SOCK!” Lillian chased him. Pop chased Lillian. Nini stood laughing.

And Trixie? Trixie sat beside the soccer bag, looking perfectly innocent. “I warned everybody,” she thought.

Finally, Boomer dropped the sock in exchange for a treat.

On the ride home, Lillian sat proudly with her two-goal smile, while Boomer and Trixie curled up together in the back seat.

Boomer dreamed of scoring his own winning goal. Trixie dreamed of a nice, quiet afternoon. And Pop? Pop was already telling everybody about Lillian's TWO AMAZING GOALS!

THE END. ❤️

And from that day forward, Boomer insisted he was Lillian's official soccer mascot. Trixie, of course, insisted she was the head coach.
