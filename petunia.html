Petunia

Flower / Annual care from planting through seasonal cleanup.

Starting

Start seed indoors 10–12 weeks before frost or purchase transplants. Seed needs light to germinate.

Planting

Set outdoors after frost in full sun and fertile, well-drained soil.

Maintenance

Water when the top layer dries, feed regularly in containers, and trim leggy growth to renew flowering.

Deadheading

Many modern types are self-cleaning, but removing spent flowers and seed capsules improves appearance.

Fall cleanup

Remove plants after frost and empty diseased container soil if necessary.
