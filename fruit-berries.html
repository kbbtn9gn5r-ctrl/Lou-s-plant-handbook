🫐Fruit & Berries

Blueberry

Acid-loving perennial berry shrub.

☀ Full sun💧 1–2 inches weekly, especially while fruiting
Open full care details

Fertilizer: Fertilizer labeled for acid-loving plants

Harvest or pruning: Pick when fully blue and berries release easily

Common problems: Birds, mummy berry, high soil pH

Lou's notes: Aim for soil pH around 4.5–5.5 and mulch with pine bark or wood chips.

🍇Fruit & Berries

Grapes

Cold-hardy grape vines trained on a support or garden arbor.

☀ Full sun💧 Deep watering during prolonged dry periods
Open full care details

Fertilizer: Light spring feeding only when growth is weak

Harvest or pruning: Prune while dormant and harvest when fully colored and sweet

Common problems: Black rot, Japanese beetles, birds

Lou's notes: Good airflow, annual pruning and removal of diseased fruit help reduce problems.

🔵Fruit & Berries

Honeyberry

Very hardy early berry suited to Maine.

☀ Full sun to part shade💧 Consistent moisture during establishment
Open full care details

Fertilizer: Light compost in spring

Harvest or pruning: Harvest when berries are blue inside and outside

Common problems: Birds, poor pollination, drought stress

Lou's notes: Use compatible varieties for cross-pollination.

Raspberry

Perennial cane fruit requiring annual pruning.

☀ Full sun💧 Consistent moisture
Open full care details

Fertilizer: Compost in spring

Harvest or pruning: Pick when berries release easily

Common problems: Cane borers, Japanese beetles, gray mold

Lou's notes: Pruning depends on whether the plant is summer-bearing or fall-bearing.

Thorny Blackberry

Once-bearing blackberry that fruits on second-year canes.

☀ Full sun💧 Consistent moisture
Open full care details

Fertilizer: Compost in spring

Harvest or pruning: Pick fully black, soft fruit

Common problems: Cane borers, Japanese beetles, orange rust

Lou's notes: Remove canes that fruited to ground level after harvest.
