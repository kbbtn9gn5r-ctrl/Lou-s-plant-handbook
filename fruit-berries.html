Fruit & Berries

Blueberries, blackberries, raspberries, grapes, and fruit-growing projects.

Blueberries

Acid-loving shrubs. Mulch, water evenly, prune older canes after establishment, protect ripening fruit from birds, and fertilize lightly with an acid-forming product.

Blackberries

Provide full sun, support long canes, water during fruit development, remove canes that have fruited, and thin new canes for airflow.

Raspberries

Grow in full sun with good airflow and support. Remove spent floricanes after harvest and thin weak canes in late winter or early spring.

Grapes

Train vines on the arbor, prune heavily while dormant, tie new shoots, improve airflow around clusters, and monitor for leaf galls and fungal problems.

Blueberry Propagation Project

Keep cuttings humid but not waterlogged, provide bright indirect light, and check for resistance before assuming roots have formed.
