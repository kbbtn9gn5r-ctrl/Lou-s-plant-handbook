Wild Ginger

Asarum species · Shade-loving woodland groundcover

Complete Care Guide

  • Plant in partial to full shade in humus-rich, evenly moist, well-drained soil.
  • Use as a woodland groundcover beneath trees and shrubs. Plants spread slowly by rhizomes.
  • Water regularly during the first growing season and during prolonged drought.
  • Top-dress with leaf mold or compost in spring.
  • Keep aggressive weeds out while the patch fills in.
  • Usually needs little pruning; remove damaged leaves as needed.
  • Leave healthy fallen leaves as natural winter mulch or apply a thin layer of shredded leaves.
  • Remove only diseased or badly damaged foliage in spring before new growth expands.
