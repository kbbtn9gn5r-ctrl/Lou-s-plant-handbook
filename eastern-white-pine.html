About this plant

A tall native pine photographed among the mature hardwoods.

Light

Full sun to part shade

Water

Deeply during prolonged drought, especially when young

Soil

Well-drained, acidic to neutral soil

Fertilizer

Usually none; compost only if growth is weak

Pruning

Remove dead, damaged or hazardous branches. Never top the tree.

Common problems

White pine weevil, needle cast, storm damage

Lou’s seasonal note: Inspect in spring for dead tips and storm damage; water during extended summer drought.
