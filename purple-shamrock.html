Purple Shamrock

Complete indoor maintenance guide for Purple Shamrock.

Light

Bright indirect light is ideal, with some gentle sun acceptable.

Watering

Water when the top of the potting mix begins to dry. Avoid keeping the pot constantly wet.

Feeding

Feed lightly during active growth.

Seasonal Growth

Growth may slow or die back during a natural dormancy period. Reduce watering until new growth resumes.
