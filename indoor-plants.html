Prayer Plant

Maranta leuconeura

A tropical foliage plant whose patterned leaves often fold upward in the evening.

☀ LightBright, indirect light
💧 WaterKeep lightly and evenly moist; do not leave soggy
🌱 FertilizerHalf-strength balanced houseplant fertilizer every 4–6 weeks in spring and summer
📝 Lou’s notesLikes humidity. Brown edges often point to dry air or mineral-heavy water.
📷 Lou’s actual plant

Jade Plant

Crassula ovata

A long-lived succulent with thick leaves and woody stems.

☀ LightVery bright light with some direct sun
💧 WaterLet the mix dry well before watering
🌱 FertilizerLight succulent fertilizer 2–3 times during spring and summer
📝 Lou’s notesRotate monthly and keep in a heavy pot. Avoid frequent watering.
📷 Lou’s actual plant

Elephant Bush

Portulacaria afra

A branching succulent sometimes called dwarf jade, with smaller leaves than true jade.

☀ LightBright light with some direct sun
💧 WaterWater thoroughly, then allow to dry
🌱 FertilizerDiluted succulent fertilizer during active growth
📝 Lou’s notesPrune leggy stems to encourage compact branching.

Spider Plant

Chlorophytum comosum

An adaptable houseplant that produces arching leaves and plantlets.

☀ LightBright, indirect light
💧 WaterWater when the top inch of soil dries
🌱 FertilizerBalanced fertilizer monthly in spring and summer
📝 Lou’s notesFiltered water may help reduce brown leaf tips.

Aloe Vera

Aloe vera

A sun-loving succulent that stores water in fleshy leaves and produces offsets.

☀ LightBright light; acclimate gradually to direct sun
💧 WaterAllow the potting mix to dry thoroughly
🌱 FertilizerVery light succulent fertilizer in spring and summer
📝 Lou’s notesUse a fast-draining mix and never leave water sitting in the saucer.
📷 Lou’s actual plant

Gasteria

Gasteria species

A slow-growing succulent with thick, often mottled or textured leaves.

☀ LightBright, indirect light or gentle morning sun
💧 WaterLet soil dry between waterings
🌱 FertilizerLight succulent feed a few times during active growth
📝 Lou’s notesTolerates less direct sun than many desert succulents.
