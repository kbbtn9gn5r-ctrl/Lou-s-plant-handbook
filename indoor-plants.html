🌵Indoor Plants

Aloe Vera

Easy-care succulent with fleshy leaves.

☀ Bright indirect light to gentle direct sun💧 Soak thoroughly, then let soil dry
Open full care details

Fertilizer: Light succulent feed in spring and summer

Harvest or pruning: Repot when crowded; separate offsets

Common problems: Root rot, sunburn, mealybugs

Lou's notes: Never leave the pot standing in water.

Open full care details

Fertilizer: Diluted succulent fertilizer 2–3 times in spring and summer

Harvest or pruning: Prune during active growth; repot every 2–4 years

Ponytail Palm

Long-lived houseplant with a swollen water-storing trunk.

☀ Bright light with some direct sun💧 Let the soil dry thoroughly between waterings
Open full care details

Fertilizer: Diluted houseplant fertilizer in spring and summer

Harvest or pruning: Remove only fully brown leaves; repot infrequently

Common problems: Overwatering, brown tips, spider mites

Lou's notes: The swollen trunk stores water, so it tolerates dryness better than excess moisture.
