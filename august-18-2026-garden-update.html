Today’s garden notes

Dahlias are flowering, tomatoes are carrying a heavy crop, King of the North peppers are setting fruit, pumpkins and carrots continue developing, marigolds are blooming, and the blueberry cuttings are continuing their hardening-off process.

Sunflower milestone: approximately 12 feet tall.

Evening sunflower update

August 18, 2026: additional photos were taken late today showing the sunflower at full height from two garden viewpoints. The flower head is now visibly swelling and beginning to open, a clear change from the earlier photo. This is being kept as a continuing progress entry because more sunflower photos will be added as the bloom develops.

Change noted: tall vegetative growth is transitioning into the flowering stage.

Plants noted today

Morning Glory — annual flowering vine; provide sun and climbing support.

Liatris / Blazing Star — sun-loving perennial and pollinator plant.

Sedum / Stonecrop — drought-tolerant late-season perennial.

Lily — allow foliage to yellow naturally after flowering so the bulb can recharge.

Pink Mallow — provisional identification; continue observing flowers and foliage before treating the ID as final.

Care notes

Columbine: flowering is finished and seed heads are present; allow seed to mature if natural reseeding is wanted.

Lavender: deadhead and shape lightly after flowering; avoid hard pruning into old woody growth.

Bleeding heart: late-summer yellowing is normal dormancy; remove foliage once fully yellow or brown.

Garden Friends · Insects

Milkweed tussock moth caterpillar: keep under Garden Friends rather than treating it as a general garden pest. It is part of the milkweed insect community.

Next care check

Continue blueberry-cutting hardening-off gradually, harvest tomatoes and peppers as they mature, watch vine crops for beetle or foliage damage, and keep documenting the sunflower as the flower head opens.
