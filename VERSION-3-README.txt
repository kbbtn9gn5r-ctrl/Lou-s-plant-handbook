WHAT CHANGED
- The 2026 photo journal is now generated from one data file: data/site-data.json.
- Plant-information buttons are controlled by the plantInfo entry beside each photo.

  2. data/site-data.json, and
  3. occasionally app-v3.js or v3.css when the design changes.
