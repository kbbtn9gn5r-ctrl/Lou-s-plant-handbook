Smoke Bush

Shrub care from planting through seasonal cleanup.

Planting

Plant in full sun for best color and flowering, in well-drained soil.

Spring care

Remove dead or damaged wood before growth begins. Apply mulch without piling it against the stems.

Pruning

Prune lightly after flowering to preserve blooms. Hard rejuvenation pruning produces large leaves but usually sacrifices flowers that year.

Summer care

Water during prolonged drought, especially while establishing.

Fall and winter

Avoid late-season fertilizing. Leave healthy structure intact through winter.
