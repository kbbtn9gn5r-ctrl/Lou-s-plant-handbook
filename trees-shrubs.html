🌳Trees & Shrubs

Flowering Shrubs

Landscape shrubs grown for flowers, foliage and structure.

☀ Full sun to part shade, depending on species💧 Deep watering during establishment and drought
Open full care details

Fertilizer: Compost in spring; avoid unnecessary heavy feeding

Harvest or pruning: Prune according to whether the shrub blooms on old or new wood

Common problems: Aphids, leaf spots, winter dieback

Lou's notes: Weigela has its own page; add other shrub names as identification is confirmed.

🌳Trees & Shrubs

Weigela

Flowering shrub best pruned immediately after spring bloom.

☀ Full sun💧 Deeply during drought and establishment
Open full care details

Fertilizer: Compost in spring; avoid heavy feeding

Harvest or pruning: Prune after flowering on old wood

Common problems: Aphids, leaf spot, winter dieback

Lou's notes: Remove up to one-third of the oldest stems at ground level when rejuvenating.
