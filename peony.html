Peony

Long-lived perennial with large spring and early-summer blooms.

Light

Full sun is best for strong flowering. A little afternoon shade is acceptable, especially during hot spells.

Soil & Water

Use well-drained soil and water deeply during extended dry periods. Avoid keeping the crown constantly wet.

Planting Depth

Keep the eyes near the soil surface. Planting peonies too deeply can reduce flowering.

Support

Large double blooms can become heavy after rain, so rings or discreet stakes help keep stems upright.

After Bloom

Remove spent flowers, but leave healthy foliage through summer so it can recharge the roots.

Fall Cleanup

After frost, cut foliage to the ground and remove it from the bed to reduce carryover of foliar disease.
