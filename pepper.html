King of the North Pepper

Vegetable care from planting through seasonal cleanup.

Seed starting

Start indoors 8–10 weeks before the last frost with bottom warmth. Provide strong light and avoid keeping seedlings waterlogged.

Transplanting

Harden off carefully and plant only after soil and nights have warmed. Space plants for airflow and use support if fruit becomes heavy.

Summer care

Give full sun, consistent watering, and light feeding. Avoid high-nitrogen fertilizer once flowering starts.

Harvest

Pick green at full size or allow fruit to ripen to its mature color for greater sweetness.

Fall cleanup

Harvest before frost. Remove plants and fruit debris, and clean stakes or cages.
