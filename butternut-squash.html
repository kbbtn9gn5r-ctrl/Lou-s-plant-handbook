Butternut Squash

Winter squash grown to full maturity for curing and storage.

Light & Space

Full sun. Give the vines plenty of room to spread and good airflow.

Water

Water deeply at soil level, especially during flowering and fruit sizing. Avoid frequent shallow watering.

Soil & Feeding

Grow in rich, well-drained soil with compost. Avoid excessive nitrogen once vines are established.

Harvest

Harvest when the rind is fully tan and hard and the stem begins to dry and cork. Pick before a hard frost.

Curing & Storage

Cure harvested squash for about 7–14 days in a warm, dry, airy place, then store in a cool, dry location.

Watch For

Squash bugs, cucumber beetles, vine borers and powdery mildew.

Maine Timing

Start indoors in May or direct sow after frost once the soil has warmed.
