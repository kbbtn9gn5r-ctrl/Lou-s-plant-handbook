Central Maine Calendar

Monthly reminders for Wayne-area gardens.

January

Order seeds, review last season’s notes, check stored dahlias and winter squash, and inspect houseplants for pests.

February

Finalize the bed plan. Start slow-growing perennial flowers late in the month if needed.

March

Start peppers around March 15–April 1 and tomatoes March 25–April 10. Prune dormant fruit canes on a mild day.

April

Direct sow lettuce, kale, chard and beets as soil becomes workable. Plant potatoes late April into mid-May.

May

Plant potatoes, harden cool-season transplants, and direct sow beans only after soil warms. Start squash and pumpkins indoors mid-month.

June

Transplant tomatoes, peppers, pumpkins and squash after frost danger and cold nights pass. Mulch and begin weekly pest checks.

July

Harvest often, succession sow beans and fall kale, monitor squash eggs and beetles, and keep berries evenly watered.

August

Harvest garlic, beans, zucchini and berries. Start fall lettuce early in the month and cure harvested onions or garlic well.

September

Harvest winter squash and pumpkins before hard frost. Reduce watering as storage crops mature.

October

Plant garlic, lift dahlias after frost, clean diseased foliage, and mulch tender shrubs after the ground cools.

November

Protect young shrubs from winter wind and browsing, drain hoses, clean tools and finish leaf mulch.

December

Review photos and journal notes, update plant records, and plan seed orders and garden changes.
