Maine Monthly Calendar

Central Maine reminders for Lou's garden.

January

Review notes, order seeds, check stored dahlias and winter squash.

February

Plan beds and order supplies.

March

Start tomatoes and peppers indoors late in the month.

April

Prepare beds when soil is workable; sow cool-season greens.

May

Plant potatoes and hardy crops; wait for warmth before tender plants.

June

Mulch, stake, feed and begin regular pest checks.

July

Water deeply, harvest often and monitor squash pests.

August

Harvest garlic, beans, zucchini and berries.

September

Harvest storage crops, cure squash and save notes.

October

Plant garlic, lift dahlias after frost and clean beds.

November

Protect shrubs, drain hoses and store tools.

December

Review the season and plan next year's garden.
