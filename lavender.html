Lavender

Sun-loving perennial herb with fragrant flowers and foliage.

Growing notes

Plant lavender in full sun and sharply drained soil. Avoid wet winter soil and heavy mulch against the crown. Prune lightly in spring after new growth begins, but do not cut deeply into old leafless wood.

Seasonal care

Water newly planted lavender until established. Mature plants prefer drier conditions. Remove spent flowers for a tidy appearance and harvest flower stems just as buds begin opening.
