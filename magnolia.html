About this plant

The large rounded specimen beside the pool.

Light

Full sun to part shade

Water

Deep watering during dry weather

Soil

Rich, well-drained soil

Fertilizer

Compost in spring; avoid heavy nitrogen

Pruning

Prune minimally after flowering. Remove crossing or damaged branches.

Common problems

Scale, leaf spot, winter bud injury

Lou’s seasonal note: Photograph flowers in spring to confirm the exact magnolia type.
